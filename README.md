# HR Analytics: Employee Attrition Analysis

## Project Overview

This project explores employee attrition and workforce characteristics using HR data. The dashboard highlights attrition patterns across departments, gender, age groups, education fields, and job satisfaction ratings.

The goal is to turn HR data into clear business insights that can support further investigation and better-informed workforce decisions.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel** — spreadsheet-based data analysis and reporting
- **Power BI** — KPI cards, interactive filtering, and dashboard visualization
- **Tableau** — data visualization and dashboard exploration
- **SQL** — querying and analyzing HR data

## Key Observations

1. The dashboard reports 237 attritions out of 1,470 employees, with an overall attrition rate of 16.12%.
2. The 25–34 age group has the highest attrition count among the displayed age groups, at 112.
3. R&D has the highest department-wise attrition count shown, at 133, followed by Sales at 92 and HR at 12.
4. The dashboard shows 150 male and 87 female attrition cases. These are counts, not gender-specific attrition rates.

These are descriptive observations from the dashboard, not proof of why employees leave. Further analysis would be required to establish causes or relationships.
